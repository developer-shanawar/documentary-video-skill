# Example Outline — Mohenjo-Daro

This is an ORIGINAL high-level outline, not a copied script. It shows how the skill is used. Dates and facts here are the ones used in the published 8:11 video "No King, No Palace, No Throne — Who Ruled Mohenjo-Daro?" — verify them again before reusing them in a new video.

- Topic: Mohenjo-daro
- Angle: "No King, No Palace, No Throne — Who Ruled Mohenjo-Daro?"
- Length: 8 minutes, built in 1-minute parts

## Hook (Part 1, Beat 1)

Counter-claim: every ancient city we know had a king and a palace. Then the question: Mohenjo-daro had neither — so who ruled it?

Visual: biggest 2D animated view of the old city first, host looking up at the board, camera slowly pushes in.

## Beats (high level, 6 per part)

1. Hook — no king, no palace, no throne. What did they find instead?
2. How advanced — drainage and water: drains under old houses, the Great Bath, planned streets. One number repeated in 3 pictures (diagram, map, real image).
3. Evidence — seals, weights, standard bricks. What these prove, and what they do not prove.
4. Clues — no grand palace, no royal tomb, no clear king's house. The absence itself is evidence.
5. Theories — council, merchants, shared rule? Labelled as theories, kept separate, not presented as fact.
6. Reality / Outro — gradual decline around 1900 BCE (not a sudden vanishing), people moved east and south, lakhon log — never a fixed invented population. Present-day ruins shown as real present-day visuals, labelled real. Payoff: a city can be organised without a king. Open question + personal sign-off.

## What this example teaches

- One angle, not a list of facts about a city.
- Evidence first, theories labelled as theories.
- Real images only where they add value, with motion, labelled real.
- Every beat has a visual that matches its voice line, and something moving in every 10 seconds.
