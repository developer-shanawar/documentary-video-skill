## Full script — 6 beats, ~10 seconds each

Beat 1 (0:00–0:10): Har purani sheher ki kahani me ek raja hota hai, lekin Mohenjo-daro me na koi mahal mila, na koi takht — to phir ye sheher chalata kaun tha?

Beat 2 (0:10–0:20): Ye sheher koi chhota gaon nahi tha, yahan lakhon log rehte the, aur har ghar ke neeche naliyon ka poora jaal bicha hua tha.

Beat 3 (0:20–0:30): Gali seedhi, eent ek size ki, aur wazan bhi ek jaisa — ye sab ek plan ki nishani hai, kisi ittefaq ki nahi.

Beat 4 (0:30–0:40): Lekin jab hum raja ka mahal dhoondte hain, to humein kuch nahi milta — na bada mahal, na shahi qabar.

Beat 5 (0:40–0:50): Kuch kehte hain mausam badla, kuch kehte hain darya ne rasta badla — ye theories hain, saboot nahi.

Beat 6 (0:50–1:00): To kya ye sheher ek din me khatam ho gaya, ya log aahista aahista chale gaye? Aap kya sochte hain?
