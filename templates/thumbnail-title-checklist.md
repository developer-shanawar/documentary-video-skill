# Thumbnail + Title Checklist

## Title

- [ ] A question or a counter-claim, not a summary. Example: "No King, No Palace, No Throne — Who Ruled Mohenjo-Daro?"
- [ ] Under 70 characters where possible, readable on a phone.
- [ ] No claim the video does not prove.
- [ ] No clickbait number that is not in the video.

## Thumbnail

- [ ] 3–4 words maximum, large and readable on a phone.
- [ ] One clear subject (host, ruin, map, or object) — not a crowded collage.
- [ ] Same colours as the video (deep teal + warm amber).
- [ ] No fake expression, no fake object that is not in the video.

## Description

- [ ] First 2 lines: angle + what the viewer will learn.
- [ ] Timestamps for each part.
- [ ] Keywords line (including tags that did not fit in the tag box).
- [ ] Sources / further reading where useful.
- [ ] Hashtags: 3–5, relevant only.

## Tags

- [ ] Total under 500 characters (YouTube limit).
- [ ] Mix of topic tags, place tags, and question tags.
- [ ] No misleading tags.
