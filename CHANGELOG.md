## v1.2.0 — 2026-11-06

Added:
- `references/thumbnail-styles-library.md` — 50 reusable thumbnail patterns in 10 groups (not copied viral thumbnails)
- `references/sound-effects-catalogue.md` — 80 named sounds by category (names + uses only, no audio files)

Honest limits, kept in v1.2:
- 500–1000 copied thumbnails and hundreds of audio files are NOT included — copying real thumbnails is not allowed, and audio files need a licence and would make the repo huge. Patterns + a named catalogue are the reusable, legal version.
- Counts above are exact and verifiable: 50 styles, 80 sounds.

Added:

- Script writing, 10-second storyboard, image/diagram prompts, voiceover script
- Video editing guide (`references/editing-guide.md`)

Honest limits, kept in v1.0:
- No fake stars, downloads, or users. No usage numbers are claimed.
- Skill does not generate video/audio/images by itself, does not watch live competitor videos, and does not upload to YouTube.
- No view, virality, or RPM promise.
