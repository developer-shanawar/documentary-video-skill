# Thumbnail Guide — Documentary Video Skill

A thumbnail has one job: make a person who already searched for the topic stop and click, without lying about the video.

## Rules

- Size: 1280x720. Text: 3–4 words maximum, large, readable on a phone at small size.
- One clear subject: host, ruin, map, or one object. Not a crowded collage.
- Colours: same as the video — deep teal + warm amber. High contrast, text white with black outline.
- Face / expression: curious, not shouting, not fake shocked. The host's expression must match the video's mood.
- No fake object, no fake expression, no claim that is not in the video. No clickbait number that is not proved inside.

## Make 3 concepts, pick 1

For every video, make 3 thumbnail concepts first, then pick the best one. Do not ship the first idea.

| Concept | Subject | Text (3–4 words) | Why it might work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — Question | Host looking at the board / ruin | NO KING? | Matches the title question |
| B — Object | One big object: seal, brick, map, Great Bath | REAL OR MYTH? | One object, easy to read |
| C — Contrast | Before / after, old vs present-day ruins | GAYA KAHAN? | Curiosity, no fake claim |

Test each concept at phone size: if you cannot read the text in 1 second, it fails.

## Thumbnail image prompt template

"2D documentary thumbnail, [subject: host / ruin / map / object] large in frame, deep teal + warm amber, clean 2D style matching the sticky-man explorer host, high contrast, no text in the image itself — text will be added in edit, white with black outline."

Text is added in edit, never baked into the generated image. If an image already has baked-in garbled text or a fake watermark, reject it and regenerate clean.

## Common mistakes

- Too many words — 6+ words become unreadable on a phone.
- Too many subjects — the eye does not know where to look.
- Colours that do not match the video — the viewer feels cheated on click.
- A shocked face for a calm documentary — tone mismatch.
- A number or claim the video does not prove.

## Checklist before upload

- [ ] Text readable on a phone in 1 second
- [ ] One subject only
- [ ] Colours match the video
- [ ] No fake claim, no fake object
- [ ] Title and thumbnail say the same angle, in different words — they do not repeat each other word-for-word
