# Sound Effects Catalogue — Documentary Video Skill
Honest note: this is a catalogue of NAMES and uses, not audio files. No sound files are included, because sounds need a licence — do not download a sound and assume it is free for monetised use. Use this list to find the right sound from a licensed library you own, then note its source + licence in your working notes.
Default rule: voiceover ONLY. Use a sound only when the user explicitly asks for sound, one sound per moment, low under the voiceover, never over a word.
Total sounds in this file: 80.
