# Fact-Check Checklist — Documentary Video Skill

Check every item before scripting. If you cannot verify it, do not present it as fact.

## Dates and numbers

- [ ] Every date is verified from a reliable source (not from memory, not from another video alone).
- [ ] Every number has a source: measurement, count, percentage, or estimate — and the source is noted in working notes.
- [ ] Uncertain populations use "lakhon log", never a fixed invented number (for example never "50 lakh" unless a source actually says it).
- [ ] A gradual change is described as gradual (for example "around 1900 BCE, gradually"), not as a sudden vanishing, unless the evidence says sudden.
- [ ] A measurement is shown the same way in the voice, the diagram, and the on-screen text.

## Names and places

- [ ] Every person's name is spelled correctly and their role/date is verified.
- [ ] Every place name is correct and located correctly on the map/diagram.
- [ ] A named person is only shown with a real image if that image is actually of them and is public-domain / freely licensed.

## Sources

- [ ] At least one reliable source per major claim in working notes (book, paper, museum, university, official site — not a random short video).
- [ ] Where sources disagree, say so on screen or in the voiceover: "kuch experts kehte hain X, kuch kehte hain Y".
- [ ] A quote is a real quote, with who said it and when. No invented quotes.

## Real vs reconstruction vs interpretation

- [ ] Every real image is labelled as real, with its source/licence noted in working notes.
- [ ] Every reconstruction is labelled as a reconstruction — never as a real photo or ancient footage.
- [ ] Every present-day photo of a ruin/river is labelled as present-day, never as ancient footage.
- [ ] Interpretation is labelled interpretation. A sacred or historical text is never claimed to name something it does not name.
- [ ] A theory is labelled a theory. Two separate causes (for example monsoon shift and river shift) are kept separate, not merged.

## Before you deliver the script

- [ ] No claim is marked "verified" in the script unless it passed this checklist.
- [ ] Disputed points are marked disputed in working notes, even if the voiceover only says "ye abhi tak ek sawal hai".
- [ ] The thumbnail and title do not claim something the video does not prove.
