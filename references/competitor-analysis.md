# Competitor Analysis — Documentary Video Skill

Use this BEFORE scripting. Watch 3–5 videos on the same topic, take notes, then write your own angle. Copy techniques, never footage, script, or narration.

## Questions to answer

1. What question does the video answer in the first 15 seconds?
2. What evidence does it show first — a document, a map, a real image, a number?
3. Where does the viewer get bored? Where does the pace drop?
4. What is missing? A counter-argument, a source, a real present-day visual, a clear payoff?
5. What is my angle in one sentence, different from all of them?

## Rules

- Analyse technique only: hook shape, evidence order, visual variety, pacing.
- Do NOT copy lines, titles word-for-word, thumbnails, footage, or scripts.
- Do NOT present a competitor's interpretation as established fact. Check it yourself — see `fact-check-checklist.md`.
- If a competitor video has no source for a claim, note it as unverified, do not repeat it.
- Keep the notes. They are part of the video's working file, and they show the maintainer did real research before scripting.

## Worked mini-example (technique only)

Reference style studied: a dinosaur-mystery video.
Techniques worth learning: counter-claim hook then a question, science first then an explicit pivot to text, one number shown in three pictures, slow zoom + blurred foreground + one big label, word-to-picture matching, open-question ending + personal sign-off, no hard subscribe graphic.
What NOT to copy: its script, its footage, its exact titles, or its personal interpretation presented as proof.
