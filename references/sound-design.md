# Sound Design — Documentary Video Skill

Default rule first, from the locked style: **voiceover ONLY**. No music, no scene sound effects, unless the user explicitly asks for sound. This file is the optional layer for when they do ask.

## When sound is OFF (default)

- Audio track 1: voiceover only, Young 3, speed 100, Roman Urdu.
- No background music. No whoosh, no pop, no ambient sound.
- Soft 0.5s fade-in on the first beat only.

Use this default for the Mohenjo-daro / Athar-style videos this skill was built from.

## When the user asks for sound — optional layer

Turn sound on only for that video, and say so in the script notes. Never turn it on silently.

| Moment | Sound | How loud / how long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screen changes to a new part | One clean transition whoosh | Short, low, once per part change only |
| A date pill or name card appears | One soft pop / tick | Very low, 0.2–0.3s, only on text reveal |
| A diagram / map is revealed | One soft reveal sound | Low, under the voiceover, never over it |
| A suspense / mystery point | BGM rises slightly, then drops | Balanced to the mood, voiceover always on top and clear |
| A payoff / lesson | BGM drops, voiceover clear, then silence for 1 beat | Let the last line land, do not cover it with music |

What NOT to do:
- No music under the whole video at the same volume as the voice.
- No sound effect on every beat — it becomes noise after 30 seconds.
- No loud whoosh, no shouting sound, no comedy sound in a serious documentary.
- No sound that covers a word of the voiceover.

## Licence — check before you publish

- Do NOT assume a sound from a free-sound site is free for commercial / monetised use.
- Use only sounds you have a licence for, or sounds you made yourself.
- Keep a list in your working notes: sound name, source, licence. If you cannot find the licence, do not use it.
- The transition whoosh used in earlier videos has an unresolved licence question for monetised use — do not reuse it commercially until that is checked.

## Editing the sound

- Voiceover track first, always. Mix music/SFX under it, never over it.
- Cut dead air over 1–2 seconds, same as the voice edit.
- If a sound distracts from a fact, remove the sound, not the fact.
- Export check: listen once on a phone speaker. If the voice is not clear there, the mix is wrong.
