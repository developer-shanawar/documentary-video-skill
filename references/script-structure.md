# Script Structure — Documentary Video Skill

## Lengths

- 1 minute: 1 part, 6 beats, 1 idea. Use for a test or a Short-style explainer (horizontal 16:9 for a long-video part, vertical 9:16 only for a Short).
- 5 minutes: 5 parts, about 30 beats. One idea per part, parts flow into each other.
- 8 minutes: 8 parts, about 48 beats. Same rule — no part labels on screen, no repeated "next part" promise.

Build in 1-minute parts, then combine at the end.

## Beat

One beat = ~10 seconds = about 25–30 Roman Urdu words at Young 3, speed 100.

Every beat has:

1. Voiceover line (spoken, Roman Urdu + balanced technical English)
2. Visual (2D animation by default, real image only with motion and only where it adds value)
3. Motion note (what moves: host walks, water flows, camera pans, zoom 100% → 108%)
4. On-screen text (date pill top-right and/or name card bottom-left for 2 seconds — at most one small text per beat)

## 5-minute structure

| Part | Time | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Hook | 0:00–1:00 | Counter-claim, then a question in the first 15 seconds. Show, do not just tell. Biggest visual first. |
| 2 — How advanced / how it worked | 1:00–2:00 | Evidence: what was built, how it worked, one number in 3 pictures. |
| 3 — Clues | 2:00–3:00 | What changed, what was found, what does not fit the old story. |
| 4 — Theories | 3:00–4:00 | Theories as theories. Keep them separate, do not merge two causes into one. |
| 5 — Reality / Outro | 4:00–5:00 | What most likely happened, what remains today (real present-day visuals, labelled real), payoff, one-line lesson, open question + personal sign-off. |

For an 8-minute video, expand parts 2–4 (more evidence, more clues, more theories) — do not add a new topic in part 7.

## Hook formulas (pick one)

- Counter-claim + question: "Sab kehte hain [common belief]. Lekin [evidence] kuch aur kehta hai — to phir asal me kya hua?"
- One number + question: "[Number] saal pehle, [place] me [surprising fact]. Ye kaise mumkin tha?"
- A question the viewer already has: "Agar [place] itna advanced tha, to wo gaya kahan?"

Do NOT use a shouting hook. Open relaxed and curious.

## Ending formulas (pick one)

- Open question + sign-off: "[Open question]? Aap kya sochte hain — comment me batayein."
- Payoff + lesson: "Is liye [lesson in one line]. Agli video me [next topic, no promise of a date]."

Do NOT end every part with "agle part mein samjhenge". End a middle part on a question only.

## What a boring script looks like (avoid)

- Same visual for two beats in a row
- A beat where the voice says one thing and the screen shows another
- A list of facts with no question, no number, and no visual change
- A 10-second segment with no motion
