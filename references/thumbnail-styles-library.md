# Thumbnail Styles Library — Documentary Video Skill
Honest note: these are 50 reusable PATTERNS, not 500 copied viral thumbnails. Copying a real creator's thumbnail is not allowed and does not help — a pattern you can reuse for your own topic does. Count below is exact and verifiable.
Rules for every style: 1280x720, 3–4 words maximum, one subject only, deep teal + warm amber, text white with black outline, readable on a phone in 1 second, no fake claim, text added in edit never baked into the image.
Total styles in this file: 50.

## How to pick one

1. Pick the category that matches your angle (Question for mystery, Number for measurement, Contrast for change).
2. Make 3 concepts from this list, fill in `templates/thumbnail-brief.md` for each.
3. Test at phone size. Pick the one you can read in 1 second.
