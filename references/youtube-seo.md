# YouTube SEO — Documentary Video Skill

From upload to engagement tracking to the next video's topic. The skill does NOT upload by itself and does NOT log into YouTube — upload and tracking are manual in YouTube Studio. This guide is what to do there.

## 1. Title — short title vs meaningful title

Make 3 titles for every video, then pick 1. A title is a question or a counter-claim, not a summary.

- **Short title (for search, under 50 characters):** "Who Ruled Mohenjo-Daro?"
- **Meaningful title (full angle, under 70 characters where possible):** "No King, No Palace, No Throne — Who Ruled Mohenjo-Daro?"
- **Test title (different angle):** "Mohenjo-Daro Had No King — So Who Was In Charge?"

Rules:
- Put the main topic words in the first half of the title.
- No claim the video does not prove. No clickbait number that is not in the video.
- Title and thumbnail say the same angle in different words — they do not repeat each other word-for-word.

## 2. Description

First 2 lines: angle + what the viewer will learn. YouTube shows only these before "more".

Template:
```
[Angle in one sentence. What the viewer will learn.]

Timestamps:
0:00 Hook
1:00 [Part 2]
2:00 [Part 3]
3:00 [Part 4]
4:00 [Part 5]

Keywords: [topic, place, question, documentary]
Sources / further reading: [where useful, real sources only]
#[Hashtag1] #[Hashtag2] #[Hashtag3]
```

## 3. Tags

- Total under 500 characters (YouTube limit). Count them before pasting.
- Mix: topic tags, place tags, question tags. Example: Mohenjo-daro, Indus Valley, ancient city, who ruled Mohenjo-daro, documentary.
- No misleading tags. No tags for a topic not in the video.
- Tags that do not fit in the tag box go in the description Keywords line.

## 4. Upload — see also `youtube-upload-checklist.md`

Upload as Unlisted first, watch fully, check text / motion / sound, then set to Public. Add thumbnail, end screen, cards, playlist, language, subtitles.

## 5. Engagement tracking after upload

Check in YouTube Studio. No view / virality guarantee — these numbers only tell you what to fix next time.

| When | Check | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First 24h | Impressions + CTR | Is the thumbnail + title stopping people? | If CTR is low, thumbnail text or title angle is the problem, not the video |
| First 48h | Average view duration + retention graph | Where do people leave? | Note the exact timecode and what visual was on screen there |
| First 48h | Comments | What question did viewers actually ask? | That question is a candidate for the next video |
| 7 days | Traffic sources | Search, Suggested, Browse, External? | If search is strong, make more search-style titles on the same topic |
| 7 days | Audience: age, country, device | Who actually watched? | Match the next topic and language to them, not to who you guessed |
| 28 days | Top videos by views | Which angle worked? | Repeat the angle shape, not the same video |

Fill in this table for every video, in your working notes. One row per video, same columns every time, so videos can be compared.

## 6. Next video topics — how to pick them

Do not pick a topic only because you like it. Pick from these 4 sources, in order:

1. **Comments on the last video:** the most-asked question becomes the next angle.
2. **Retention drops:** the beat where people left is a topic you did not answer well — answer it properly next time.
3. **Search:** what did people search to find your video? (YouTube Studio > Reach > Traffic source: Search). Make the next video on the top search term you have not covered yet.
4. **Competitor gaps:** from `references/competitor-analysis.md` — what is missing in their videos? That gap is your next angle.

Write 5 candidate topics, score each 1–5 on: search demand, evidence available, visual variety possible, your angle is different. Pick the highest score. Keep the other 4 in a list for later.

## 7. What the skill gives you for SEO

- 3 title options per video (short, meaningful, test)
- Description template with timestamps, keywords, hashtags
- Tags within 500 characters
- Thumbnail text + prompt (see `references/thumbnail-guide.md` and `thumbnail-styles-library.md`)
- Engagement tracking table above
- Next-topic scoring list above

What it does not give: automatic upload, live analytics, or a promise of views.
